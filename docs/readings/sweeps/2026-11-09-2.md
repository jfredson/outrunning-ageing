1. **2026-11-06. The United States drug regulator is investigating cancers in children given the gene
   therapy Zolgensma.** Its notice says three reported tumours involved one cancer gene, and in two of
   them the therapy's own DNA was found inserted beside or inside that gene. It says the same risk
   applies to the second product built on the same carrier, that it is watching other gene therapies
   carried by the same kind of virus shell, and that the benefits still outweigh the risks.
   - Address: https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/safety-availability-biologics/fda-investigating-serious-risk-malignancy-linked-zolgensma-gene-therapy
   - Kind of source: regulator (the page is dated 2026-11-06).
   - For or against: against, for every treatment carried into the body by the same kind of virus shell.
   - Record: no record of its own. It bears on "A person receives cell-resetting genes in a trial"
     (`a-person-receives-cell-resetting-genes-in-a-trial`) and "Gene therapy lengthens chromosome ends in
     an old animal" (`gene-therapy-lengthens-chromosome-ends-in-an-old-animal`), which use the same
     kind of carrier.
   - Held: no. The premature-ageing entry names Zolgensma only as an example of an approved gene therapy.
   - Why it matters: a regulator has confirmed that this kind of carrier put its DNA beside a cancer
     gene in a person, and the cell-resetting and chromosome-end gene therapies rest on it.

2. **2026-11-07. The full ZEUS results were presented: the antibody ziltivekimab, which acts only on
   inflammation, made no difference to heart attacks, strokes or heart deaths.** In 6,376 people with
   artery disease, kidney disease and raised inflammation, 17.6 per cent on the drug and 17.7 per cent
   on placebo had such an event (hazard ratio 0.99); serious infections were more common on the drug
   (20.4 against 17.9 per cent).
   - Address: https://www.hcplive.com/view/ziltivekimab-fails-to-reduce-cardiovascular-events-in-ascvd-and-ckd-zeus
     and https://www.statnews.com/2026/11/07/aha-2026-conference-novo-nordisk-zeus-trial-ziltivekimab-results/
   - Kind of source: press reports of a meeting presentation and a journal paper (the paper itself was
     not opened; see "Seen but not opened").
   - For or against: against.
   - Record: "A drug that acts only on inflammation is approved to prevent heart attacks"
     (`an-anti-inflammatory-drug-is-approved-to-prevent-heart-attacks`).
   - Held: yes, as the paper by Ridker and colleagues (`ridker-2026`).
   - Why it matters: it was the largest test so far of treating inflammation alone, and the marker
     moved while the disease did not.
