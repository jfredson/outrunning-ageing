# How the headline is computed

Written 2026-10-16 (weekend step 2 of `docs/2026-10-16-kickoff.md`) and **PROPOSED, not yet ruled**. The method itself (every leaf carries a range for the year it reaches the clinic; the tree is played out many thousands of times; each run's escape year is the latest handled year across the required branches plus a lag; the middle of the runs is the headline, the share before 2050 the second number, the share after 2100 the third) was proposed in the kickoff and accepted by John as written on 2026-10-16. The **parameters** below were proposed by Claude with a confidence level and the strongest alternative for each, per the ground rule of 2026-09-08, and wait on John. The code is `scripts/compute.py run`; the result it writes is `data/headline.json`. Until the leaf records exist, `run` says so and stops (the readiness rule, item 8).

## What the computation does

1. **Every tracked leaf supplies a year it reaches the clinic** (`in-clinical-use`). A leaf already there, or beyond, supplies the year it happened. A leaf below draws a year from its estimate: a triangle over the earliest, likely and latest years, so the likely year is the most probable draw and the ends are possible but rare (`docs/record-schema.md`, "How a leaf's state feeds the headline").
2. **Every branch counts as handled in the year its `handled_when`-th leaf reaches the clinic.** With the default of two, a branch is handled when its second treatment is in clinical use. The branch's leaf years are sorted and the second is taken.
3. **The run's escape year is the latest handled year across the branches marked `required`, plus a drawn lag** for the treatments to spread from the clinic to ordinary people, so that the year is when a person who keeps up with the best available care actually gets the gain, not when the paper came out.
4. **Many thousands of runs**, each with fresh draws, give a spread of escape years. The headline reads three numbers off it: the median year; the share of runs with an escape year before 2050; the share of runs with an escape year after 2100, said in public as "not this century". The 5th and 95th percentiles are reported as the spread ("anywhere from 2044 to not this century" when the 95th is past 2100).

Two honesty rules from the kickoff, repeated on the public method page: the combination rule (all required branches handled, plus a lag) is the project's judgment, and the strongest alternative, a weighted sum in which some branches matter more, is recorded below with why it was not chosen; and the ranges are the project's reading of the evidence, revised only with a dated reason and a source, never because a number looked wrong.

## A worked run, by hand

For the method page, once the leaves exist, one simplified run is written out so a reader can follow it: eight branches, two leaves each, one drawn year per leaf, the second-smallest year per branch, the largest of the eight, plus the lag. Until then this is the shape of it:

| branch | leaf years drawn this run | handled (second leaf) |
|---|---|---|
| Worn-out cells | 2036, 2041 | 2041 |
| ... | ... | ... |
| Measuring ageing | 2033, 2047 | 2047 |
| latest handled year across the required branches | | 2047 |
| plus a drawn lag of 9 years | | **2056**, this run's escape year |

## The parameters (proposed 2026-10-16, ruled by John the same day; parameter 2 went to the outside review of 2026-10-17 and stays as ruled)

| # | Choice | Confidence | Standard or judgment | Strongest alternative and its cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The draw for a leaf below the clinic is a triangle** over the rung's `earliest`, `likely` and `latest` years. | Moderate. Three numbers are what a person can argue about in public ("no earlier than, most likely, no later than"), and the triangle is the simplest shape that uses all three and nothing else. | Judgment; a common choice where only three-point estimates exist. | A bell curve centred on `likely` with the ends two standard deviations out: smoother, but it never says "no earlier than", and the earliest year on a treatment with no human result is a real floor. Or a probability per year, as The Belt Equation carries: more expressive, harder to argue about. |
| 2 | **The lag from the clinic to ordinary people is a triangle over 3, 8 and 15 years**, drawn once per run and shared by every branch. | Low on the numbers; moderate on the shape. New drugs take years to reach wide use after approval (price, guidelines, supply); the figures are a first guess with no source. **The outside review of 2026-10-17** (`docs/reviews/2026-10-17-rulings.md`, items 1 and 2) was asked for published figures and produced none that checks out: the four sources the reviewer named fail on opening (a PMC address holding a COVID-19 review in place of a drug-sales study; a review in Brain that could not be found; a 17-year figure, Morris and colleagues 2011, that measures discovery to practice and says approval to use is unmeasured; and one figure with no source named). Three of its four answers called 3, 8, 15 defensible for ordinary drugs; one proposed **5, 10, 20** for every branch, which would give a headline of 2061 and 0.73 per cent before 2050. **Declined by John on 2026-10-17:** no sourced figure supports a change, so the numbers stay a first guess, and the real next step is a reading task, published uptake curves (time from approval to half of eventual use) by kind of treatment. | Judgment. | A lag per branch, drawn separately: more honest about the difference between a pill and a transplant, at the cost of eight more guesses. **What the review suggested (2026-10-17), as four proposals, all without a source for any year:** the cells packet, 5, 10, 20 with a pill scaling in 5 to 8 years and gene and T-cell therapies taking 15 to 20 years or more; the DNA packet, a lag per kind of treatment with no years given; the fuel packet, 3, 8, 15 kept for energy and fuel and **8, 15, 25 for replacing parts** (surgery, bespoke cells and animal organs need facilities, training and matching networks); the signals packet, **1, 3, 5 for measuring ageing and 5, 10, 15 for inflammation and the body's messaging** (a cleared blood test scales at once through central laboratories; plasma exchange and microbe transfers need chairs, donors and clinic time). Scratch runs with every other branch at 3, 8, 15 gave: the fuel proposal 2058 and 0.91 per cent before 2050; the signals proposal 2058 and 1.48 per cent; the cells proposal 2061 and 0.73 per cent; all four together 2060 and 0.30 per cent; and 3, 8, 15 drawn separately per branch instead of once per run 2058 and 3.35 per cent, the same as the shared draw. **Declined for now by John on 2026-10-17:** a lag per branch needs eight sourced numbers and the review produced none; it returns when the uptake reading has figures by kind of treatment. If the structure is wanted sooner, it is a small change to the engine (a lag drawn per branch and added before the maximum is taken), and the numbers can start at 3, 8, 15 for every branch, which leaves the headline where it is. Or no lag, with the clinic rung as the arrival: simpler, and it would count the year a drug is approved as the year everyone has it. |
| 3 | **All required branches must be handled** (the latest handled year is taken). | Moderate; the kickoff's rule. It says in the model what the hallmarks papers say in prose: ageing is many kinds of damage, and outrunning it means dealing with each. | Judgment. | A weighted sum, in which the branches with the most deaths behind them count for more and a branch could be skipped: it would pull the year in and would need eight weights nobody has published. Recorded here, as the kickoff asks, with why it was not chosen: a weight is a second judgment on top of the ranges, and the ranges are already the arguable part. |
| 4 | **The fixed dates are 2050 and 2100.** The chance before 2050 is the second number; the share after 2100 is "not this century". | High on 2050: the kickoff's reason stands (the loudest promises land before it, and most readers under fifty expect to see it). High on 2100 as the century's end. Both are project constants, changed only at a yearly review and logged. | Standard: the kickoff's. | A reader's own life expectancy (The Belt Equation's framing), deferred to version two as the kickoff says. |
| 5 | **200,000 runs.** | High. The percentages then wobble by about a tenth of a point from seed to seed, far below any rounding the site shows, and the run takes under a second. | Standard: "many thousands" with margin; the same as The Quiet Galaxy. | One million runs for a twentieth of a point, or 20,000 for noise that would show in a diff of the JSON. |
| 6 | **A fixed seed, 2004** (the year the phrase "longevity escape velocity" became known). | High that the seed is fixed: the published numbers then reproduce exactly on any machine, and `data/headline.json` changes only when a record changes, so every change to the headline is a visible diff with a changelog line. Low on the particular value, which can be any number. | Standard practice for a published computation. | No seed: the numbers wobble on every run and every build produces a diff. |
| 7 | **Independent draws**: every leaf's year and the lag are drawn without any link between them. | Moderate. Nothing published gives a rule for how a breakthrough in one branch speeds another, and a rule invented here would be a third judgment. **Raised at the outside review of 2026-10-17** (`docs/reviews/2026-10-17-rulings.md`, item 3): all four packets, each with high confidence, said the measuring branch should be linked to the treatment branches, because a regulator accepting a marker of ageing as proof would let every treatment's trial read out in years rather than decades. The two sources that exist, Barzilai and colleagues 2016 (`barzilai-2016`, the TAME trial's design paper) and Moqri and colleagues 2023 (`moqri-2023`, the consortium paper on what a marker must do), argue the dependency and do not measure it; the record already carries the link in words (the measuring branch is marked required, and five treatment records give the marker's acceptance as the premise of their earliest year). Illustrative scratch runs with a shared factor explaining a quarter, a half or three quarters of each draw's variation left the middle year at 2057 or 2058 and raised the chance before 2050 from 3.35 to 9.8, 14.9 and 19.5 per cent. **Ruled by John on 2026-10-17:** no link is implemented, because the size of it has no source and a size invented here would be a third judgment; the argument stays on the record here so the next review of the method starts from it. If the structure is wanted, the honest form is a rule in the engine's own terms (in a run where the measuring branch is handled before a stated year, each treatment branch's draw is taken from the earlier part of its range), with the year and the shift given a stated reason. | Judgment. | Linked draws (a shared "the field is going well" factor): more realistic, since the branches share money, regulators and tools, and it would widen the spread; it needs a parameter with no source. |
| 8 | **The readiness rule: `run` refuses until every branch has at least `handled_when` tracked leaves**, naming the branches short of it. | High. Without that many leaves a branch can never be handled and every run would say "never", which would be the shape of the record and not of the world. | Judgment, following the siblings' "stub refuses until the factors exist". | Treat a short branch as handled at its last leaf's year, or skip it: either hides the gap. |
| 9 | **The result is written to `data/headline.json` and committed**, and `scripts/export.py` reads it. | Moderate. It is data the site reads, it belongs with the data it is computed from, and committing it means a headline change is a reviewable diff. The same choice The Quiet Galaxy made and John ruled there. | Judgment. | Compute at build time inside the export script with no file: nothing to commit, but the headline could change without a changelog line. |
| 10 | **Years past 2300 are refused in the record; a drawn year past 2100 counts as "not this century"** and is otherwise left as it is in the spread. | Moderate. "Never" has to be expressible, and a far year is how a three-point estimate says it; 2300 is far enough that nothing is lost. | Judgment. | A separate "chance it never happens" field per leaf: cleaner in meaning, one more number to argue about, and it needs a rule for combining with the years. |

## What the headline file holds

When `run` works, `data/headline.json` carries: the date it was computed; the method block (runs, seed, lag, the fixed dates, the status of this document); the median escape year; the chance before 2050; the share after 2100; the 5th, 25th, 75th and 95th percentile years; a histogram of escape years by five-year bin for the site to draw; for each branch its median handled year and its leaves' years; and the counts of estimates still at first pass and of claims still to verify. Two counts of first-pass estimates are given: `estimates_first_pass` is every estimate the leaves carry, including the wide-use estimates on leaves already in the clinic, and `estimates_first_pass_drawn` is the part the headline actually draws from (a leaf already in the clinic has a known year, so its estimate is shown on its page but not drawn). The export script reads it; the site shows "not computed yet" in words until it exists.
