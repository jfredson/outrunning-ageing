"The rulings, item 13" is now "an outside review of the site's evidence". - **Rule 3 and the family's ban on project words in posts.** "Rung", "tree", "handled", "session", "reading" (as a noun for the weekly search) and "record" (as the site's word for its files) are replaced: a stage, the site, dealt with, the site's automated weekly search, the treatment's page. The five stages and the turning point are named once, before any number, in each post.
- **Rule 2, every referent gets its noun.** "The year at the top" is tied to the turning point it estimates; "an anti-inflammatory drug" is named as colchicine (`data/leaves/an-anti-inflammatory-drug-is-approved-to-prevent-heart-attacks.toml`); "a liver virus's markers" is "the signs of a liver virus, hepatitis B" (`data/leaves/a-cells-settings-are-rewritten-at-one-gene-in-a-person.toml`, estimate notes).
- **Rule 7, the everyday word.** "No significant lengthening" is "no lengthening that could be told apart from chance"; "sham procedure" is glossed; "trial registry" is "the public register of clinical trials"; "third-quarter results" is "quarterly results".
- **Rule 8, facts kept.** The supplier is "the company that supplies the programme" (the record's "the supplier Grifols"), not a narrower claim about what it supplies. "Tests of daily functioning" keeps the record's "tests of daily function". The wide-use estimate's reason is the record's own ("assumes wide uptake waits for a second controlled trial").
- **One reason for the year holding.** The two posts gave different reasons; both now say the year waits for the slowest groups and inflammation was not one of them, which `data/headline.json` history bears out (inflammation 2042, slowest 2044 before and after the decisions of 2026-10-17).

For John: the plasma post now speaks in the first person ("I ruled"), as the Start here post already did, where the old post said "John ruled". The kind label on posts and their cards now reads "a new stage reached" instead of "a rung reached". The Start here post runs to about 1,500 words, above the 800 to 1,200 that rule 4.13 gives for an opener, because it now names the stages and explains the two hundred thousand runs before the numbers; the plasma post is about 830 words with links, near the top of rule 4.19's guide.
