## Facts, each against its source (opened 2026-11-27)

**The eGenesis and Mass General Brigham release of 2026-11-23**, read in full on eGenesis's own page in the
browser pane:
- Dated November 23, 2026; Molly Falco, "a 58 year-old female", kidney failure from a condition diagnosed in
  childhood, dialysis since 2024.
- Transplanted November 22, 2025, with EGEN-2784, at Massachusetts General Hospital, "under the U.S. Food and Drug
  Administration's (FDA) Expanded Access pathway".
- "she remains free from dialysis"; "the longest sustained function of a genetically engineered porcine kidney in
  a living human recipient".
- Three classes of edits: endogenous retroviruses inactivated; three glycan antigens removed "to reduce the risk of
  hyperacute immune rejection" (the post: "reject the organ almost at once"); seven human transgenes "to regulate
  immune response, reduce inflammation, improve coagulation compatibility and regulate complement activation"
  (the post: "calm the immune response and inflammation and ... help the blood clot normally").
- "To date, six patients have received EGEN-2784 through the FDA's Expanded Access pathway." The release gives no
  outcome for the other five, which the post says.
- RESTORE: a Phase 1/2/3 trial in 33 patients aged 50 to 70 waitlisted for a human kidney, start expected in the
  first quarter of 2027.

**eGenesis's release of 2025-09-08** (opened): the FDA cleared its IND to begin a clinical trial of EGEN-2784.

**United Therapeutics' quarterly report** (opened through the SEC with a named client):

- The UThymoKidney IND was cleared in August 2025.
