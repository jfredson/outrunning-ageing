## 2026-10-16

- Founding publications log: 37 records added under `data/papers/`, making 38 with the Hickson 2019 worked example, every one checked against the paper, release or registry entry on the web the same day. The founding papers (the two hallmarks papers, rapamycin in old mice, the two senescent-cell clearance papers, partial reprogramming in mice, the optic nerve paper, Horvath's clock, the 2024 demographers' case against radical life extension, de Grey's 2004 "escape velocity" essay), the trial record (Unity's 2020 failure from the company's own release as filed with the SEC, the TAME trial's design paper and its funding history as the sponsor's own page gives it, the Dog Aging Project's two papers, the 2025 PEARL rapamycin trial, Life Biosciences dosing a first person on 2026-06-09) and the key results behind the other leaves (metformin, GLP-1 drugs, young plasma and plasma dilution, NAD precursors, telomerase gene therapy, immune rejuvenation, mitochondrial transfer, pig heart, iPS-cell retina, lab-grown bladder, clocks as trial measures, senolytics in lung scarring). Every record names the branch it bears on and, in its notes, the leaf it is meant to be wired to; `moved_range` and `settled_stage` are false on every new record until the leaves cite them. Four corrections the checking turned up are written into the records: de Grey's 2004 paper coined "actuarial", not "longevity", escape velocity, and no 2004 primary source for David Gobel's coinage exists, so his own 2022 account stands in (`gobel-2022`); the Bannister 2014 metformin ratio is printed the other way round from how it is usually quoted; the Ocampo 2016 lifespan gain of "30 percent" is from a press release, not the paper; and no primary source says the TAME trial has begun enrolling, while its sponsor's page as of 2026-07-04 still asks for donors to launch it. Eleven claims across seven records remain in verify lists, mostly figures read from secondary sources where the paper is behind an access wall. The Hickson 2019 record's two verify items were checked against the open-access text and cleared, and a note on its 2020 corrigendum added.
