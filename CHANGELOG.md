Four corrections the checking turned up are written into the records: de Grey's 2004 paper coined "actuarial", not "longevity", escape velocity, and no 2004 primary source for David Gobel's coinage exists, so his own 2022 account stands in (`gobel-2022`); the Bannister 2014 metformin ratio is printed the other way round from how it is usually quoted; the Ocampo 2016 lifespan gain of "30 percent" is from a press release, not the paper; and no primary source says the TAME trial has begun enrolling, while its sponsor's page as of 2026-07-04 still asks for donors to launch it. Eleven claims across seven records remain in verify lists, mostly figures read from secondary sources where the paper is behind an access wall. The Hickson 2019 record's two verify items were checked against the open-access text and cleared, and a note on its 2020 corrigendum added.
- **The leaves are written: 43 in all, three to seven per branch** (worn-out cells 5, damaged DNA 4, the cell's settings 5, broken proteins and waste 5, energy and fuel sensing 7, replacing lost cells and parts 7, inflammation and the body's messaging 5, measuring ageing 5), each one file under `data/leaves/` with a plain name, a test two readers would agree on, a settler, the stage with its date and public record, earlier stages where known, a horizon, and a first-pass estimate for every remaining rung as earliest, likely and latest years with the reasoning written out. Five carry a `sibling` link to The Upgrade Tree's lifespan-and-repair branch, with the same test in substance and the same stage: a drug makes people live longer in good health; partially resetting cells reverses ageing in a living animal; a drug clears aged cells from a person; an organ from a gene-edited pig keeps a person alive; a printed or lab-grown organ works in a person for a year. Six leaves are already in the clinic (an antibody that clears amyloid from the brain, 2023; a drug that stops a misfolded protein settling in the heart, 2019; a weight-loss drug approved to prevent heart attacks, 2024; grown cartilage cells for a worn joint surface, 2016; a drug acting only on inflammation approved to prevent heart attacks, 2023), one is a long shot (a human organ grown inside an animal) with its mechanism and breaking point, and three are at idea only. Every date, figure and link written from memory is in the record's verify list: 135 entries to clear across the records. Public records are cited by link; the publication ids are wired at the merge with the papers session's log.
- **The first headline is computed.** `scripts/compute.py run` now does what `docs/method.md` says (a triangle draw per leaf below the clinic, a known year per leaf in the clinic, a branch handled at its second leaf, the latest required branch plus a lag of 3 to 15 years, 200,000 runs, seed 2004) and writes `data/headline.json`: the middle escape year is **2058**, the chance before 2050 is **2.7 per cent**, and the share not this century is **0.0 per cent**, with the middle nine tenths of runs between 2051 and 2068. By branch, the median year each counts as handled: broken proteins 2023, replacing parts 2029, energy and fuel 2039, damaged DNA 2040, measuring ageing 2040, inflammation 2042, worn-out cells 2043, the cell's settings 2044. The spread is narrower than the kickoff's example because a branch is handled by its second-fastest leaf, so a branch's slow leaves never set its year; that is the handled-at-two rule doing what it says, and it is written up for the outside review. Six new tests cover the computation (reproducibility with the fixed seed, known years read rather than drawn, the floor on the escape year); 67 tests pass.
