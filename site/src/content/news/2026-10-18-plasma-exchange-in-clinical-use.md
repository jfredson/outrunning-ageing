## What happened

On 27 April 2026 the Ace Alzheimer Center in Barcelona [published on its own page](https://www.acebarcelona.org/en/plasma-exchange-alzheimers-asfa-2026) that 71 people with Alzheimer's disease had completed its fifteen-month plasma-exchange programme "under routine clinical practice conditions", presented at the American Society for Apheresis meeting of 2026. The [weekly reading of 17 October](/reading/#2026-10-17) had found a supplier's press release saying the programme has run since 2021, and left it for the author, because a supplier's release is not the clinic's own publication of its practice. An outside review the same day brought the centre's own page, and John ruled on 17 October that it counts ([the rulings, item 13](https://github.com/jfredson/outrunning-ageing/blob/main/docs/reviews/2026-10-17-rulings.md)).

## Why

The treatment's test says the clinic rung is reached when a regulator approves the procedure for an age-related condition, or when a named clinical setting gives it for that purpose in routine care and publishes that practice ([the treatment's page](/leaves/replacing-a-persons-plasma-slows-an-age-related-disease/)). The centre's page is the second of those: a named setting, routine care, published by the setting itself. The supplier's press release of 12 March 2026 was not enough on its own, because the rule asks the clinic to say it, and a rung never moves down, so a mistake could not be undone. The rung is dated to the centre's publication, not to 2021, when the supplier says the programme began, for the same reason.

## What it does not mean

It does not mean plasma exchange slows ageing. The programme treats Alzheimer's disease, one age-related condition, and the [trial behind it](https://doi.org/10.1002/alz.12137) (347 people, reported in 2020) moved some of its measures and not others. It does not mean the procedure is approved for ageing anywhere; no regulator has said so, and the apheresis society's guidelines place it in the category whose role is not yet established. And the middle year did not move: the year is computed from the branches' slowest treatments, and this one was not on that path ([the method](/method/)).
