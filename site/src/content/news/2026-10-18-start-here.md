## The idea

Ageing is not one thing. The site breaks it into eight branches, each a kind of damage that repair would have to address, and under them forty-four specific treatments and measuring tools ([the tree](/branches/)). Each treatment carries a plain test of what would count, a public record of how far it has got on a five-rung ladder from idea to wide use, and a range of years for when it reaches the clinic, with the reasoning beside it. The tree is played out two hundred thousand times, and the middle of all those runs is the year at the top of the home page ([the method](/method/)).

## How to follow it

Once a week a reading searches the news against every record and writes down what it found; the home page's strip says when it last ran and whether anything moved ([this week's reading](/reading/)). When something moves, a post appears here the same day, saying what happened, what it changed, why, and what it does not mean. The [feed](/news/feed.xml) carries every post; [the story so far](/story/) carries every change, including the ones too small for a post.

## The most interesting news so far

Plasma exchange moving into clinical use on 27 April 2026, found by the reading of 17 October and ruled the same day ([the post](/news/2026-10-18-plasma-exchange-in-clinical-use/)); and the outside review of 17 October that re-argued every treatment's years and moved the chromosome-ends drug later because its one trial was stopped as futile ([the story](/story/#logged-2026-10-17)).

## What to watch for

A regulator approving any treatment on the tree for an age-related condition; a second treatment on the inflammation branch reaching its clinic rung; the first of the measuring tools being used to run a trial rather than to describe one ([the tree](/branches/)). Each would move the year, and each would get a post.
